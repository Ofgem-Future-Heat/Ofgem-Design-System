Ofgem footer

The Ofgem footer appears at the bottom of every page and provides links to legal and policy pages including Accessibility and Cookie policy.

When to use this component

Use on all Ofgem digital services. The footer must include the Accessibility statement link and the Cookie policy link.

When not to use this component

Do not omit the footer from any page. Do not replace it with a custom footer that removes the required legal links.

How it works

The footer sits in a light grey band at the bottom of the page. It contains two links (Accessibility, Cookie policy) on the left, and the copyright notice on the right: © Ofgem 2026.

Accessibility

Ensure footer links are keyboard accessible and the content information landmark role is applied to the footer element.
