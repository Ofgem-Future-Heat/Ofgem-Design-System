About Ofgem Design System

The Ofgem Design System defines the foundations of user interfaces across Ofgem's digital services. It brings together all Ofgem product and service experiences under a single, unified framework — ensuring consistency, accessibility and coherence across every service we deliver.

Use the Ofgem Design System alongside the GOV.UK Design System and the MOJ Design System .
